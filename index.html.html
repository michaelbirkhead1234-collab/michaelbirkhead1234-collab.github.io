Academic studio · MSU Bozeman
A-101Mission Rock, San Francisco

Sanctuary of Generations

Mixed-use housing that pairs affordable units for young adults and seniors around a shared courtyard inspired by the Haveli, with communal kitchens, mentorship programs and ground-floor retail. Its proximity to UCSF anchors the site choice.

I carried the project through full construction documentation: concrete wall sections, foundation detailing, green roof drainage and wood-to-concrete connections, with a timber structure and locally sourced materials. I also tested fabric-formed concrete, drawing on Omer Arbel's House 75.9.

  • Intergenerational housing
  • Construction documents
  • Timber + concrete
A-102San Francisco

Mission Rock Master Plan

A sustainable framework for Mission Rock that weaves mixed uses, green infrastructure and pedestrian-first streets together. I produced the zoning and vegetation plans for the parking garage, community park and ferry terminal sites in Rhino and Illustrator, with biodiversity and stormwater management leading the decisions.

  • Urban master plan
  • Green infrastructure
  • Rhino · Illustrator
A-103Peet's Hill, Bozeman

Live-Work-Play Community

Affordable student housing that combines a grocery, classrooms, a restaurant and dormitories in one radial, site-responsive form. Reverse solar envelope massing and passive strategies drove the iterations, which moved from a rectilinear scheme to a radial one for better circulation and a stronger sense of community.

  • Student housing
  • Solar envelope
  • Passive design
